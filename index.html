01 / THE QUESTIONFROM SIGNALS TO DECISIONS

How much life
is left?

Can sensor and operating data help us estimate when an engine will reach the end of its useful life?

Our goal is to compare simple baseline predictions with classical regression models using NASA’s N-CMAPSS aircraft engine degradation dataset. We’ll measure prediction error in flight cycles and explore what it could mean for maintenance planning.

Real flight operating profiles. Simulated engine degradation. An academic study, with no operational aviation validation.
